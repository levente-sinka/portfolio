About the Creator · Levente Sinka

Photo and video that sell the experience of your space.

I'm a photographer and videographer working with hospitality venues and real estate. I'm based in Ghent, where I study International Business Management at Artevelde University. I think about what your content needs to achieve, not just how it looks.

Quick shoots, no disruption to your staff or guests. The final edited photos and videos arrive on your phone within 3 to 4 days. You run the business. I make sure it looks undeniable on screen.
